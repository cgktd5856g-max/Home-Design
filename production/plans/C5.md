## The Advent Stair

This is the upgrade list for the advent_drawer_wall session (rooms/advent_drawer_wall, branch claude/video-advent-wall, commit d33a0d1). That session has already built the Advent Stair, and its layout is locked, so do not rebuild it. Everything below that is not marked **Change** already exists in its PLAN.md and project.py: keep it.

**Pitch**: An 8 m² kids' room builds around a loft bed. The six-step stair on the east wall hides 24 drawers: 21 in the stepped side face and 3 in the top risers. They open bottom to top, each on the next note of a rising F major scale. Each tray slides out, its front drops into a little ledge, and a tiny colourful treasure hops onto it. Then they all snap shut. The child climbs, opens the last drawer, and its star flies to the tree on the loft as the lights come on.

- **Deadline.** [inference] The README gives about 35 s per frame on 4 cores: about 3.6 h for the 15 s cut (375 frames) and 10.7 h for the long cut (1,100 frames). The production HANDOFF.md gives 17-26 s per frame at 720x1280 and 16 spp: 1.8-2.7 h and 5.2-7.9 h.

- **Safety debate.** The stair is 1.38 m high and open on the room side. Its 230 mm rises are steeper than UK and US house-stair rules allow (about 220 mm and 196 mm) [fact, building rules; not in the research files]. The three riser drawers open over the tread below.
  - Add the oak guard and the fixed window.
  - Keep the child off any tread under an open drawer.
  - Pin the plan and call it a loft-bed stair.
- **Colour drift.** [measured] Off-band colour marked 13 of 25 flops against 2 of 25 hits, and white-heavy renders fall low (our kitchen v2: saturation 0.11).
  - Use light-oak fronts, neutral window light and the gold duvet.
  - Run hh_metrics on the low-res preview before any Blender time.
  - If warmth is still under 22, recolour the rug a soft mustard. If it tips over 57, or the frame looks yellow, go back to the linen duvet.
