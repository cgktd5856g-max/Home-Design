## The Floor Steps Up

**Pitch**: The back of an empty 9 m² bedroom lifts a metre into a bed platform with a drawer stair. Its front then gives drawers, a desk, and finally a made-up second bed for the friend who just walked in.

**Post caption** (in the post, never on screen): "9 m² bedroom, 2.8 × 3.25 m (about 9 ft 2 in × 10 ft 8 in). A bed, a desk, a wardrobe, and a bed for the friend who stays over. The back of the floor went up a metre, and its front kept going. Desk by day, guest bed by night: would you climb a metre to bed every night, or take the pull-out? Send this to the friend who always stays over."
- December variant: "...a bed for the friend staying over for the holidays." No festive props.
- Pin a plan with dimensions and "Modelled and rendered from code, no AI video, no design app."

**Why it should work**
- **Bedroom plus a size is the strongest support** [measured]:
  - Bedrooms run 1.51× normal, and titles naming a size 1.34×.
  - Only two 2026 raised-bed titles exist, and both won: "Be bold! Raise the bed by 90 centimeters..." (343K, 10.39× normal, date est. 31 May 2026) and a raised kids' bed (289K, 7.81×, est. 7 Sep 2026).
  - The report says all 4 best 2026 videos (290K-547K) had a raised or loft bed. The frame notes in deep_analysis.csv confirm 3 of them (547K loft, 343K, 289K). The 536K one is a "conventional layout".
  - The best and worst 2026 videos had the same pacing (motion 7.7 against 7.9), so subject and format made the difference.
- **Short works only if it never stops** [measured]: "Two Beds in One Small Room with Smart Wardrobe & Pull-Out Storage" (June 2025) got 991,555 views (5.16×) in 24 s. It had the highest motion of all 59 (15.5), and its longest still was 0.6 s. This is a model for the 15 s cut, not proof the premise still works.
- **The premise is not proven in 2026, so it is the send line, not the claim**:
  - [measured] In 2026, Haven Haus's two-kids titles ran a median of about 0.70×. One of the 5 worst 2026 videos is a two-kids room (17,591 views, 0.49×).
  - [measured] Only 2 Haven Haus titles mention a guest room (0.70× and 0.83×). No data supports a "friend stays over" premise.
  - [fact, search snapshot, indicative] The only 2026 two-beds data point: HomeCraft's "Two kids, one room" (22 May 2026), 22.5K likes against 260-924 for its other posts.
- **It follows the top-5 structure** [measured]:
  - The top 5 open on the empty room (4 of 5), have 6-12 storage reveals, and show a 3D person using a feature (4 of 5).
  - The most praised moment in the 19.3M kitchen is a person stepping on a pull-out drawer. A top comment calls "the pull-out box doubling as a stool" genius.
  - [inference] Here: 10 storage moments, a stair whose risers are drawers, and a friend sitting on a bed that came out of the same front as the desk.
- **It is built to be sent and saved**:
  - [fact] Instagram's head says sends count slightly more for content that reaches non-followers.
  - [fact] Content that inspires awe is shared more (Berger & Milkman, 2012).
  - [inference, trends file] Originals with real dimensions and believable mechanics stand out against AI-looking content. The pinned plan gives people a reason to save it.
  - [inference] "Where does the friend sleep?" names someone to send it to. A made-up bed rolling out of a desk front in 9 m² is the awe beat.

  - `figure()`, `walk()`, `track()` and `sit_pose()` from `figure.py`;
  - `music.py` at 120 BPM.
- **New pieces**:
  - the platform top slab, which lands as the far row of planks and then slides up 1.0 m while the birch carcass grows beneath it;
  - three stair blocks with riser drawers, cubbies and felt bins, plus the grab rail;
  - 4 shallow and 2 wide drawer fronts;
  - the pull-out desk with a fold-flat lamp;
  - the made-up trundle (frame, castors, mattress, duvet slab);
  - the stair-top lid with a shallow, lit, white bin (a box suitcase and a pillow);
  - the niche column and books, the guard, the slat rails and their items (a helmet from a half ellipsoid, a cap, a tote);
  - the backpack, with a small custom transform that follows the friend's torso until it drops;
  - two short sound effects in `music.py`: a rumble (a low, filtered noise swell) and a boing. Fallback: thud plus whoosh, and chime.
- **Effort**: medium. It is almost all boxes with slides, rises and hinges. The figure, its pelvis blend and the backpack are the only new kinds of work.
- **Hard to render, and how to avoid it**:
  - **Duvets**: flat `cloth()` slabs with a settle wobble. No cloth simulation and no fold-to-flat scaling.
  - **The figure**: keep it faceless, with no hair.
  - **Window**: a roller blind instead of sheer curtains, and an emissive sky card instead of outside geometry.
  - **The mirror door**: if it adds noise, keep it out of the longest shots.
- **Render time**: the README gives about 35 s per frame on 4 CPU cores at 720x1280. That is about 11 h for the 1,150-frame long cut, plus about 3.6 h if the 375-frame 15 s cut is rendered on its own. The camera never stops, so few frames can be copied.
