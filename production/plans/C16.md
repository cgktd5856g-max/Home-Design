  - S1: (1.0, 0.25, 1.2, -6, +14) → (1.45, 1.5, 2.9, -5, -16)
  - S2a: (2.2, 0.6, 1.4, -110, -8) → (2.0, 1.4, 2.4, -55, -10)
  - S2b: (1.35, 0.7, 3.7, -4, -18) → (1.45, 0.9, 4.2, 0, -24)
  - S3: (2.15, 1.5, 4.55, -90, -6) → (2.15, 1.3, 1.25, -90, +4)
  - S4: (1.3, 0.6, 1.5, +25, +4) → the frame-1 pose (1.0, 0.25, 1.2, -6, +14)
  - HOLDS: (0, 3.0), (3.3, 5.0), (5.3, 7.0), (7.3, 10.7), (11.0, 15.0).
  - Probe S2b and the loop pose first. The landing, the wardrobe and the whole bed must read in S2b, and the final wide must show the kitchen, the loft and the top of the library.
  - Put both cuts in one project and switch between them with an environment variable (CUT=15 or 49).
- **Effort**: high. It has hundreds of books, ten drawer rigs, a pod, a kitchen, a fan, fanlights, a door, a ladder, a carried box, a four-action figure and about 1,225 Cycles frames in the long cut. To stay lean, build it in stages:
  1. Build the 15 s cut with only the pop and stand. Use merged book rows and the kitchen rig as it is.
  2. Render the 3 s colour tests, then the full 15 s cut, and check it with `hh_metrics.py`.
  3. Add the long-cut choreography (walk, climb, reach, sit) only if the 15 s cut passes and looks right. The long cut takes about 3 times as long to render.
- **Hard to render, and how to avoid it**:
  - Water: the shower stays dry and no tap runs.
  - Hair and faces: the figure is the faceless `figure.py` mannequin.
  - Complex fabric: only the `cloth()` duvet; garments are `garment()` boxes.
  - Glass: plain window panes and the frosted pod door and panel only. No glass shower screen, no glass balustrade.
  - Noise in the tall space: add a fill light near the ceiling and use adaptive sampling.
