## Count the Beds

**Pitch**: A whole room for two kids, with a raised bed, builds on a podium. The guests' bags skid in through the open door at 0.3 s. Then more beds come out of the podium. Each sleeper's reading light clicks on with a note one step higher, until 10 m² sleeps five and everyone can still reach the door without climbing over a bed.

**Post caption** (in the post, never on screen): "Everyone says skip the podium in a small room. This one hides 2 beds. 10 m² (3.4 × 3.0 m, about 11 × 10 ft), 2 kids + 3 guests for the holidays. Would you give grandpa the pull-out or the top bunk?"
- Evergreen long cut: the same, but end the second sentence with "2 kids + 3 when the family stays over".
- Pinned comment: the night plan with dimensions; "Every sleeper reaches the door without crossing a bed."; "Modelled and rendered from code, no AI video."

**Why it should work**
