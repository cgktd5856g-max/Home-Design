### Pitch

Eight coloured plates land in a ring on the floor of an empty kitchen. The kitchen builds around them. Then its slim island stretches into a table for eight in three clacks, and eight folding chairs, each seat matching a plate, hop out of an oak wall that looked like plain cabinetry.

### The room

- Size and shape: 11.5 m², a 3.2 × 3.6 m rectangle, with a 2.6 m ceiling.
  - **West wall (long)** holds the whole kitchen: fronts 0.60 m deep, worktop 0.62 m deep. From south (door) to north (window):
    - a 0.45 m tall pull-out pantry;
    - a 0.6 m fridge column, with a vent grille in the plinth and a gap above (this answers the "no room to vent" complaint, 167 likes). The two tall units stand together by the door;
    - a 0.6 m sink unit with pull-out bins;
    - a 0.45 m slim dishwasher;
    - a 0.6 m "dinner column": a pegged plate drawer over a pan drawer; above the worktop a lift-up tumbler cabinet, and a top cabinet with a pull-down dish lift holding the platter;
    - a 0.7 m oven and hob with a slim hood, with the dinner column's 0.6 m of worktop as its landing space;
    - a 0.2 m spice pull-out.
    - Upper cabinets run to the ceiling. The splashback is clay.
  - **East wall (long)**: flush light-oak panels 0.15 m deep that read as plain cabinetry.
    - The middle 2.3 m (y 0.65-2.95 m) hides eight folding chairs, hung flat in a 4 × 2 grid behind two bi-fold doors (four leaves of about 0.575 m).
    - When open, the folded leaves stand at y 0.65 and 2.95 m, about 0.25 m clear of the east-end chairs.
    - The south 0.65 m is a broom cupboard.
    - The north 0.65 m is open shelving, 0.25 m deep, for cookbooks.
  - **North wall (short)**: a 1.2 m window centred on the table, sill at 0.95 m, snow outside.
  - **South wall (short)**: a 0.85 m doorway on the same centre line, with a pocket door, so no door swings into the room.
  - **Middle**: a slim island with a 1.2 × 0.85 m oak top at dining height (0.76 m) on a 0.14 m steel-cored spine. A folded leaf hides under the top. Extended, it becomes a 2.0 × 0.85 m table.
  - **Measured plan (pin it)**:
    - Table at x 1.45-2.30 m, y 0.80-2.80 m.
    - Three seats a side at 0.67 m spacing, plus one at each end.
    - Clearances: 0.83 m from the table edge to the worktop (0.85 m to the base fronts), 0.75 m to the chair wall, 0.80 m at each end.
    - Knee room: about 0.35 m each side of the spine, a little under the usual 0.40 m.
    - With the island closed, the cooking aisle is 0.83 m: one cook at a time.
    - Say the trade-offs openly: "Seats eight, seated. Cook first, then sit: once everyone is in, the oven and dishwasher stay shut and nobody passes the host. The seat by the door gets up for latecomers."
- Palette:
  - Shell: clean white walls and ceiling (not cream); matte white upper cabinets and tall columns; pale-grey worktop; floor of pale white-oak planks.
  - Wood: light oak base fronts, island and table top, chair frames and the whole chair wall (the biggest surface in the wow shots).
  - One accent: a muted clay splashback, kept to a strip behind the run.
  - Colour comes from what fills the storage:
    - eight plates, eight tumblers and eight chair seats in eight muted glazes (sage, celery, dusty blue, slate, clay, ochre, plum-grey, butter), so each chair matches a plate;
    - bowls, mugs and tea jars in the uppers; pantry jars (lentils, pasta, rice, beans, tea) and coloured spice lids;
    - cookbooks; one red and one sage enamel pot;
    - oranges and green apples; a small potted fir with a few red berries.
  - No readable text on any prop: plain colour-block book spines, unlabelled jars, a blank oven display, no appliance badges, plain knobs.
  - Avoid walnut, warm-white or cream fronts, red-and-gold, and patterns. The runner is plain linen.
  - Targets: saturation 0.17-0.38 and warmth 22-57. Our kitchen v2, with a similar white shell, measured 0.11 and 14.5.
- Light:
  - Daylight: soft, bright snow light through the north window, low warm sun on the snow outside, and a warm fill (about 3,500-4,000 K) from the doorway. Aim for brightness of about 150-165 (the band is 138-178).
  - Lights-on (12.2 s in the short cut, 42.2 s in the long cut): a white dome pendant over the table, LED strips under the upper cabinets and in the open shelves.
  - Outside, the snow turns to a warm low-sun dusk while the room stays bright (not a cool blue dusk). Grade the pendant so warmth stays at 57 or under.
