### Pitch

A 3.4 × 2.7 m kids' room builds around a loft bed and a wall of colourful books, with one coral book sticking out. A basket tips and toys spill across the floor. A child tugs the coral book, and a section of the bookcase, still full of books, unlatches. The child pushes it back and slides it aside, like a pocket door, onto a bright den under the loft. Then the mess flies in: blocks into a bench chest, everything else into cubbies. The floor is clear and the child climbs in.

### Why it should work

  - Kids' rooms run at 1.35× the channel norm (48 Shorts), and titles naming a size at 1.34× (15 Shorts).
  - The best 2026 upload is a kids' room (853,468 views, Aug 2026). Second is a bedroom (689,706). Third is a small kids' room with a loft (547,264).
  - All 4 "recent_top" 2026 videos in the sample (290K-547K) are bedrooms or kids' rooms that report.md codes as raised or loft beds; the CSV frame notes confirm 3 of the 4.
  - [fact, platform-reported] Xiaohongshu's 2026 home trends name three things this room builds: kids' toe-kick step drawers, storage at a 1 m child's eye height, and a "mess-tolerant zone".
- [measured] **A door that opens, used by a person, is proven.**
  - The 8 m² kids' room (5,673,214 views): a child draws on a chalkboard door, then it opens.
  - The 12.8M loft: a hidden door opens onto the closet at 9-11 s. Separately, commenters retell a mum vanishing into the wall at the start of the video.
  - "Tiny but Magical — A 6㎡ Bedroom with a Secret Space!" ran at 4.83× normal (396K).
  - A 3D person uses a feature in 4 of the top 5. Three sampled flops also show a person, so a person alone does not separate hits from flops.
- [measured] **A whole room, and a camera that never stops.**
  - 28 of 29 hits build a whole room, against 12 of 30 flops.
  - The hit's camera moved more in 21 of 25 pairs: motion 8.4 against 5.1 per 0.2 s, longest still 1.4 s against 2.6 s.
  - Short works only if it never stops: the 24 s two-beds room (991,555 views) had the highest motion of all 59 (15.5).
- [measured] **Hidden rooms flop when they are warm and still**: 60,853 views (warmth 110.7, longest still 3.2 s) and 56,848 (warmth 68.4, longest still 4.2 s).
- [measured] **But our own renders run too cool**: warmth 18.3 and 14.5, saturation 0.11, against the hits' 22-57 and 0.17-0.38.
  - [inference] So the den gets warm-neutral light from inside, every station glides, and warm books are on screen by 1.5 s. Aim for warmth 30-40.
- [inference] **The payoff is storage, not a secret.** The top 5 have 6-12 storage reveals. Here the hidden den's job is to swallow the floor mess, which turns the reveal into the hits' own language and earns the January slot.
- **Sends, rewatches and comments.**

  - [snapshot, n=1] One AI concept video of four chairs tucking into cabinetry drew 288 comments on 5,717 likes (5.0%), the highest comment rate among genre rows with 1,000+ likes. Weak evidence.

### Sound

- **Landings**:
  - 'thud' for the deck, mattress, stair boxes, wardrobe, desk and bench lid;
  - 'tick' for planks, books (a fast riffle during each sweep), toys and crayons;
  - 'swish' for every drawer and door;
  - 'chime' for the lights coming on;
  - a short, bright 'thud' for the runners, track and handrail clank.
- **Two playful effects**:
  1. The tug: a crisp latch click (a high 'tick'), then a slow wooden creak with a soft runner roll as the section slides (8.05-9.9 s in the 15 s cut, 27.6-29.5 s in the long cut).
  2. The tidy-up: each toy flies in on a rising plucked note, so the mess becomes a quick upward run that ends on the lid's thud (9.95-10.55 s and 29.6-31.2 s). The long cut's cubby sort reuses the run, softer.
  - [measured] Commenters notice playful sound: "the Scooby-Doo sound effects" drew 214 likes.
- **Music**: an original cue at 120 BPM.
  - [measured, weak] Hits had a median of 121 BPM against 100 for flops.
  - Drop the drums for one beat before the tug, so the click lands in a hush.
  - [measured] No need to cut on the beat: 23% of the hits' scene changes land near a strong sound, against 24% by chance.

### Risks and fixes

- **The den goes dark, orange or grainy**, like the two hidden-room flops (warmth 110.7 and 68.4).
  - Fix: a soft area-light panel inside the den (3500-4000 K), a hidden fill behind the inside-den camera, no warm bulbs in the den, and more samples or denoising on those stations.
  - Check stills at 34.0 s (den open, child sitting) and 36.0 s (inside): brightness 138-178, warmth 57 or under, no visible noise.
- **The whole video comes out too cool and grey**, which is this pipeline's usual failure (warmth 18.3 and 14.5, saturation 0.11).
  - Fix: sun at about 5000 K, interior LEDs at 3500-4000 K, lamps at 3000 K; birch and light oak; warm books filling at least a quarter of S1.
  - Measure stills at 2 s, 9 s and 14 s (blue hour) of the 15 s cut before the full render. Aim for warmth 30-40.
- **It repeats the slate.** #4 Advent Stair is a kids' loft with stair drawers and a book-wall den. #1 Tape Line has a loft, stair drawers and a loaded wardrobe that swings on a post. #9 Count the Beds is a 3.4 m-wide kids' room with a west door and a raised sleeping zone on the east.
  - Fix: no stair drawers (the risers are book cubbies), no headboard ledge or torch, a coral accent, a storage payoff instead of a secret, a section that slides aside like a pocket door instead of swinging, and a plan mirrored against #9 with its own frame 1.
  - Check the block-out side by side with the #1, #4 and #9 plans before modelling.
- **Room proportions** (I changed the review's example):
  - The review suggested 2.7 × 3.4 m with the loft on the short wall, which would copy #4 Advent Stair's shape (2.6 × 3.1 m, loft on its short north wall).
  - I kept the short-wall loft but turned the room: the camera looks down the 3.4 m axis at the book wall, and the stair runs along a long wall.
- **Title words** [measured]: titles with "secret", "hidden" or "magic" run at 0.82 (11 Shorts). A Sep 2026 kids' room titled "…It even has a secret space!" got 17K views (era-adjusted 0.459; date estimated).
  - Fix: keep those words out of the caption and hashtags, and lead with "9 m²" and "Pull one book and the floor is clear".
- **A "fake or dangerous" pile-on.**
  - Fix:
    - No spring. The book releases a latch, and the child pushes the section back and slides it aside by hand over about 1.8 s.
    - 2 cm shelf lips, a finger-guard strip on the section's leading edge, and floor runners and a top track you can see, which land early with a clank.
    - Keep the loft rail, the 0.40 m way up and the stair handrail.
  - The section never moves into the room: it goes 0.3 m back into the den, then slides behind the fixed south run. Nothing sweeps the floor, the desk or the child's path.
  - Pin the top-view plan with the slide path, then let the debate run.

So the Xiaohongshu toe-kick step drawer goes in the wardrobe plinth, and the stair foot keeps a plain 0.50 m landing.
- **No close-and-reopen from inside, in either cut** (I changed the original):

- **Cliché, or a single feature in disguise** [measured, dates estimated]: kids' rooms ran at 0.71 in 2026 H2 (9 Shorts).
  - Fix: the den is one of seven zones and nine storage moments; the reveal comes at 60% and pays off in storage.
  - Post 6-15 Jan, at least 5 weeks after #4 Advent Stair. [fact, secondary, indicative] "Declutter" searches spike in early January.

  - Shared `materials()`, `build()` and `lights()`; two HOLDS lists and two timelines.
  - Preview and render the 15 s cut first.
- **Reuse from `rooms/bedroom/project.py`**:
  - the birch and oak materials, plus the linen, mattress and pillow materials. Swap the warm-white wall for neutral white;
  - `garment()`, `stack()`, `plant()` and `pillow()`;
  - the books loop and the `from_camera` sweep;
  - `drawer_fn()` (with `base=`) for the desk drawer and the toe-kick drawer; `door_z()` for the wardrobe pop and slam; the bed `lift()` hinge for the bench lid.
    - These three are closures nested inside that project's `timeline()`, so copy them into the new file; they cannot be imported;
  - the light slots (`rect_light`, `spot_rect`, `sphere_light`, the sun spot), the `bd_day`/`bd_night` backdrop swap and the BLENDER dict.
- **Reuse from `rooms/kitchen/project.py`**: the pull-out pantry pattern (the carcass stays and the contents fly in once it is out), for the cart and the toe-kick drawer.
- **Shared helpers**: `camkit.hold_camera` for the 14 long-cut holds (13 stations plus the final wide) and the 4 short-cut holds; `figure.py` for the child.
- **New pieces**:
  - the deck and coral rail;
  - the book stair, with its five riser cubbies and handrail;
  - the bookcase:
    - the fixed runs, with the parking pocket behind the south run;
    - the 0.85 m section on floor runners and a top track, on an L-shaped path (0.3 m back, then 0.7 m south);
    - the finger guard, shelf lips and latch;
  - the books grouped by shelf section and by cubby, with the clue book as its own piece;
  - about 30 plain toys, animated in about 6 flight groups;
  - the den: pad, cushions, the bench chest and its two lids, six cubbies and the ceiling light panel;
  - the wardrobe tower and its toe-kick drawer;
  - the desk, drawer, cart, chair and lamp;
  - the south window, roller blind, rug, hooks, backpack, print and plants;
  - two new sound kinds in `music.py`: 'creak' and a rising pluck run (built from the existing `pluck()` and `whoosh()`).
- **Must-checks**:
  - The section's books and the clue book use the section's transform (section(t) @ their own landing). Otherwise they float when it slides.
  - In top view, the section moves only inside the wall line and the den (x 0.65-1.20, y 0-1.55): it never enters the room, so the desk, the rug and the toys (which land at x 2.4-2.8, y 1.45-2.0) are untouched.
  - Parked, it sits at x 0.65-0.90, y 0-0.85, clear of the den pad (from y 0.90) and the bench.
  - The clear opening is 0.70 m (y 0.85-1.55). That is enough for the child, but not for a camera to follow the child through.
  - Cameras:
    - S3 and D1 stay about 2 m from the book wall while the section moves;
    - the swings into and out of the den (34.3-34.6 s and 37.7-38.0 s) pass through the 0.70 m opening, at least 0.1 m from either edge, and must clear the parked section; E as written starts beside it, so move E north if the probe shows a clash;
    - probe with ROOM_REST, plus frames at those two swing midpoints.
  - Inside the den, keep the camera below 1.35 m so it never clips the deck.
  - No lettering anywhere.
  - Give it its own camera path, music track and accent, new against `rooms/bedroom`, `rooms/kitchen`, #1 Tape Line, #4 Advent Stair and #9 Count the Beds.
- **Effort: medium.**
  - 1,138 frames, and the long cut takes about 3 times the 15 s cut's render time (report section 10).
  - On top of that come the figure's walk, reach, edge-tracking push and sit, the inside-den shot, about 260 books and the toy flights.
  - If the render budget runs short, cut E and stretch D2.
- **Hard to render, and how to avoid it**:
  - **Fabric**: no cloth draping; use `pillow()` and rounded boxes.
  - **Hands**: no close-ups. The book tilts as the hand arrives, and the tug is shot medium-wide from about 1.7 m.
  - **Den noise**: use the panel light and the fill, plus more samples or denoising on D2 and E.
  - **Piece count**: group the books by section and cubby, and the toys by flight group.
