# Two kids, one 9 m² room — plan

Idea #1 from `research/trends_nov2026.md` ("two kids, one 9 m² room, no bunk bed, privacy split"), with the
structure of `research/next_videos.md` idea 1. Evidence: kids' rooms 1.35× normal views, "two kids, one room"
1.36×, size in the caption 1.34×; Haven Haus's 8 m² kids' room (5.7M) and two-kids room (4.3M) are both top 7.

Caption (for the post, never on screen): "2 kids, 1 room, 9 m². No bunk bed. Which side is yours? 👇"

## The room

2.6 m × 3.5 m × 2.7 m = 9.1 m². A narrow room seen from the door in the south wall, one tall window at the far
(north) end with a sunbeam down the middle. The two halves are mirror images about the centre line x = 1.3,
so most pieces land in **mirrored pairs at the same instant** (two landings at once).

| | West half — child A (celery accent) | East half — child B (soft-blue accent) |
|---|---|---|
| Bed (y 1.45-3.5) | **raised bed**, deck top 1.30 m, over a **kid-height walk-in wardrobe** (entry 0.5 m wide in the deck's south face) | **low bed** 0.32 m with two big under-bed drawers facing the aisle; **overhead cabinets** with lift-up flaps above it |
| South of the bed (y 0.85-1.45) | **stair drawers**: four steps (0.26 m risers) up to the deck, each a drawer that pulls out into the aisle | **tall column cabinet** with a **toe-kick step drawer** that pulls out as a step |
| Near corner (y 0.05-0.8) | desk + two shelves + chair | mirrored desk + shelves + chair |
| Centre line | **sliding slatted oak partition**: three telescoping panels parked as a thin fin in front of the window mullion; they slide out to split the bed zone (y 1.45-3.5) | |

Centre aisle 0.7 m wide with a long rug. Ceiling track for the partition.

## Palette (colour band)

- Shell: white walls (very slightly cool, never cream), white ceiling, light oak floor.
- Furniture: white lacquer carcasses, light oak (fronts, deck rails, partition slats, desks).
- Accent A: muted celery green (≈ sRGB 186,198,160) — duvet, pillows, desk chair, a few drawer interiors.
- Accent B: muted soft blue (≈ sRGB 160,180,202) — the same pieces on the east side.
- Colour from the filled storage: clothes, toys, books, bins in soft mixed colours (sage, blue, blush, mustard,
  cream, navy, terracotta), never saturated. No orange cast, no dark wood.
- Night finale: warm 3000 K LEDs, but the shell stays bright (downlights on).

## Storage moments (numbered)

1. **Stair drawers** (west): four step drawers pull out one by one, fill (shoes, folded tees, books, toys), shut.
2. **Toe-kick step drawer** (east column): the plinth pulls out as a step, fills with blocks, shuts — mirrored
   with stair drawer 1 so the two open at the same instant.
3. **Under-bed drawers** (east, two): slide into the aisle, toy bins drop in, slide shut (mirrored with stair
   drawers 3-4).
4. **Overhead lift-up flaps** (east, two): lift, storage boxes fly in, slam shut.
5. **Walk-in wardrobe under the raised bed** (the wow): LED on, rail drops in, garments slide on one by one,
   shoe shelf, folded stacks, boxes — seen straight down the tunnel.
6. **Desk drawers** (both desks, mirrored): open, fill (pencil tray, notebooks, crayons), shut.
7. **Shelves + books above both desks** (mirrored, books fly from the camera).
8. **Tall column cabinet** (east): door pops open, hooks of clothes fill, door slams (long cut).
9. **Bed-head niches** on both beds: book, lamp, plush ball (long cut).
10. **Sliding slatted partition** closes between the halves: the final beat, then the lights.

Person moment: child A (a faceless mannequin in the celery tone, `fg.figure(..., 'child')`) climbs the stair
drawers step by step; child B (soft blue) sits on the low bed. Both are there when the partition closes.

## Long cut (~45 s)

Same opening, then a new zone every 6-10 s and a new angle every 2-4 s; wow at 60-70 % of the runtime.

| Time | Zone | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3.0 | doorway hero | identical to the 15 s cut |
| 3.3-9.5 | desks (reverse angle, then closer) | desks, shelves, books, chairs, lamps; desk drawers mirrored |
| 9.8-16.0 | stair ⟷ column | stair drawers ⟷ toe-kick + column door (clothes on hooks); child A climbs the stairs |
| 16.3-22.5 | low bed (east) | under-bed drawers fill with toy bins; overhead flaps lift, boxes, shut; bed-head niche |
| 22.8-31.0 | walk-in wardrobe (**wow ~27-30 s**) | LED, rail, garments, shoes, stacks, boxes; deck niche + string of lights on the raised bed |
| 31.3-36.0 | raised bed top | bedding details, reading lights; child A sits on the deck, child B on the low bed |
| 36.3-45.0 | doorway, offset → frame-1 pose | partition telescopes closed; dusk; lights cascade; ≥2 s drifting wide that loops to frame 1 |

Selected by `ROOM_CUT=long`; `build()` is shared.

## Sound

Original synth cue from `pipeline/music.py` at 122 BPM. A sound on every landing (thud for big pieces, tick for
small items, swish for drawers and doors, chime for lights). Playful touches: a rising run of ticks as garments
slide on, three wooden clacks for the partition panels.

## Owner's rules (check before every render)

No on-screen text, labels, letters or numbers on props, no end card; no characters or logos; original audio
only; clean and bright; white shell, light oak, accents muted.
