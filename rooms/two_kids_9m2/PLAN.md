# Tape Line, Turning Wall — two kids, one 12 m² room

**Concept update (coordinator, 23 Nov):** this slot switched from the original "slatted partition, 9 m²" plan to the
judged winning concept C2, "Tape Line, Turning Wall" (`production/sessions/two_kids_9m2_update1.md` on the
coordinator branch). The folder and branch names stay `two_kids_9m2`. The first version (slatted partition) is
kept as `project_slatted_v1.py` for reference only.

**Pitch:** a two-tone tape line zips down the middle of an empty kids' room. Two different-but-fair halves build at
the same time. At bedtime a loaded double-sided wardrobe swings 90° off the far wall on a steel pivot post, locks
exactly on the tape, and telescopes into a wall: one playroom becomes two bedrooms. Then it swings back for the loop.

**Caption (post only, never on screen):** "2 kids, 1 room, 12 m². One playroom by day, two bedrooms by night. Which
side is yours? Send this to your sibling." Pin a top-view plan showing the arc, the pivot post and the casters.
**Debate:** keep it shared or split it? The loft or the den?

## The room (3.6 × 3.4 × 2.7 m = 12.2 m²)

Door centred in the south (near) wall, onto a short hallway. One window in each side wall. Mirror axis x = 1.8 (the tape).

| | West: child A (celery) | East: child B (soft blue) |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | **loft bed**, deck at 1.6 m (x 0-1.0, y 1.4-3.4), slatted rail, ladder on the east face | **mid-sleeper**, deck at 0.9 m (x 2.65-3.6, y 0.05-2.0), slatted rail |
| Under the bed | under-loft desk + drawer, lamp, stool | **den** with a blue door: cushions, picture books, a ball; LED inside |
| Storage facing the door | **book wall** (4 shelves) on the loft's south face | **4 stair drawers** (blue fronts) rising north along the bed's west side |
| Desk | under the loft | small desk + shelf north of the bed, outside the swing arc |

**The turning wardrobe:** 1.5 m long × 0.5 m deep × 2.2 m tall on five casters, parked against the north wall
(x 1.55-3.05). Steel pivot post floor-to-ceiling at (1.8, 3.15) and a curved steel floor track (r 1.12 m). It swings
−90° about the post into the NE quadrant and lands centred on the tape (x 1.55-2.05, y 1.9-3.4); a 0.8 m end panel
telescopes out to y 1.1, leaving a shared entry strip by the door.
- Face A (celery, faces the room when parked, faces A when swung): two drawers, two doors over folded shelves, a
  right column with boxes, and the **hatch** at child head height (a small door on each face).
- Face B (blue cubby grid, against the wall when parked, faces B when swung): 12 fabric bins + books.
- Arc check (top view): swept radius 1.27 m. Clearances: B's bed NW corner 1.43 m, B's desk corner 1.38 m,
  floor lamp 1.58 m from the post; nothing in the arc during either swing; no figure is ever inside the arc.

## Palette

White shell, light-oak floor and bed frames, white carcasses. The only accents are muted: celery for A (wardrobe face
A, loft bedding, desk drawer, stool, rug A) and soft Cottage-Door blue for B (face B grid, stair-drawer fronts, den
door, bedding, rug B). Colour from books, toys, bins and clothes. Tape: half celery, half blue. No pink, no
saturated theme, no dark wood.

## Storage moments

1. **Book wall** fills left to right, column by column (by ~3.5 s).
2. **B's stair drawers** open in a wave bottom → top, fill (shoes, tees, blocks, crayons), close top → bottom.
3. **The den**: door opens, LED on, cushions + picture books fly in, door slams.
4. **Wardrobe face A drawer** opens and fills (just before the swing).
5. **The swing** (wow): whoosh, latch clunk, end panel telescopes out.
6. **Face B cubbies**: 12 bins + books fill in a cascade, only reachable once swung.
7. **Both desk drawers** fill at the same moment, mirrored (long cut).
8. **Face A doors** open, folded shelves shown, doors close (long cut).
9. **Hatch**: small doors open on both faces; a book passes through (long cut).
10. **Play table** lid lifts, toy bins drop in (long cut).

**Person moment:** two faceless child mannequins (`figure.py` 'child'): A in celery, B in soft blue. 15 s: A sits on
the loft, B on the mid-sleeper at bedtime. Long cut: they play at the table on the rugs, walk to their own sides, A
climbs the ladder, B climbs the stair drawers; through the hatch one passes a book to the other.

**Believability:** the pivot post and floor track land with metallic clanks at 1.3 s, the carcass slams at 1.55 s,
and at 2.1-2.6 s it **nudges 18° on the post and settles back** (teases the payoff). Casters visible.

## Sound

Original synth cue (`pipeline/music.py`, 122 BPM). Zip for the tape, slaps for the planks, double thud for the beds,
metallic clank for the post/track, a whoosh + latch clunk for each swing, swishes for drawers, chimes for lamps.

## Owner's rules

No on-screen text, labels, letters or numbers on props, no end card; no characters or logos; original audio only;
clean and bright; white shell, light oak, accents muted.
