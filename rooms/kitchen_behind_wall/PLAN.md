### Pitch

A 14 m² studio holds a storage bed and a plain birch wall. On frame 1 the wall folds away, and a kitchen builds and fills behind it and on the east wall. Then lids drop over the hob and sink, the run becomes a desk, and a person sits down to work. In the 15 s cut the wall folds shut, the studio is calm again, and the loop opens it once more. The long cut ends on the lit, finished room.

### Sound

- **Landings**: every piece gets a sound.
  - `thud`: panels, carcasses, worktop, lids, stool, bed drawers.
  - `tick`: tiles, jars, fruit, bowls, books.
  - `swish`: doors, drawers, the corner shelves, the sink cover.
  - `chime`: the lights.
- **Playful effects**:
  - The wall opens on four rising wooden clacks (0.16-0.40 s) and, in the 15 s cut, closes on four falling ones (13.90-14.40 s), so they bookend the loop. Make them as higher-pitched thuds (`thud` events with a raised `f0` override), which needs no new code.
  - The long cut's pull-down comes down with a soft "pfff" (a `swish`) and springs back up with a small cartoon "boing" (34.0 s). The boing needs one new sound kind in music.py, which has only thud, tick, swish and chime. Commenters notice playful effects: the "Scooby-Doo" comment has 214 likes.
- **Music**: original, at 120 BPM (set `{"bpm": 120}` in music.json).
  - The long cut is exactly 23 bars.
  - The 15 s cut is 30 beats (7 bars plus 2 beats), so the loop restarts on a downbeat at 0.0 s. The first clack follows at 0.16 s.
  - Cuts don't need to hit the beat: 23% of the hits' scene changes land on a strong sound, against 24% by chance.

### Risks and fixes

- **It could read as a remake** of the 19.3M kitchen.
  - Fix: no step drawer, appliance flap, slide-out table, bottle cupboard or person reaching a top cupboard. The wow is a beat that video never had (the run becomes a desk). The wall folds; it doesn't crack.
  - Birch, a sage stack-bond tile and a birch floor: no walnut, no checker floor.
- **The category is weak right now**: 2026 kitchens ran 1.03, against 1.32 for kids' rooms and 1.37 for bedrooms, and none passed 129K.
  - "Whole small home" titles are a gamble too: 5 Shorts at 0.70, though they include the 12.8M loft and the 5.5M flat. That flat's title also drew the channel's biggest title fight (a 2.3K-like comment).
  - Fix: lead the caption with the true size and a claim the video proves. Post it as a kitchen test, not in a best slot. No dining zone, sofa or TV (living or dining 0.70).
- **Frame 1 is the closed studio, not an empty room.** 22 of 29 hits open on the empty room and 4 on a finished room, the 19.3M kitchen among them.
  - Kept because the seamless vanish loop needs the bed in both the first and last frames. The room still moves at 0.10 s, and the kitchen side is empty behind the panels.
- **Grey, or too warm**: our kitchen renders measured saturation 0.11 and warmth 14.5, against targets of 0.17-0.38 and 22-57. A big birch wall and warm contents can tip the other way.
  - Fix: a neutral sun, a birch floor, and blue, teal and green contents. Check saturation and warmth on probe stills at 0.0 s and 9.8 s first.
  - Then run `hh_metrics.py` on both cuts. Treat a miss as direction, not a gate: only 9 of 29 hits pass every target (6 of 29 with the length target). The median hit misses 1, the median flop 5 (5.5 with length).
- **Dark or grainy recesses** (the larder, the blind corner, under the uppers).
  - Fix: the constant hidden fills plus the niche fill. Probe 0.0, 8.0, 9.8 and 12.8 s at final settings (18 samples) before rendering everything.
- **Practicality pile-ons**:
  - "Where does the steam go?": the duct rises into the bulkhead and, in the pinned plan, turns east to an outside grille. There is no slot in the bulkhead's face.
  - "Where do the panels go?": the pocket is shown.
  - The fridge: an 80 mm filler, a hinge away from the corner, grilles from frame 0, and the door opening fully in the long cut.
  - "No oven?": the caption doesn't claim a full kitchen.
  - Sitting at a counter: the 0.25 m knee ledge.
- **A broken loop (15 s cut)**.
  - Fix: the figure is hidden at 10.75 s, the stool is back by 11.45 s and the sink cover by 11.15 s. Everything else that changed sits behind the panels.
  - The tall return and its grilles exist from frame 0, the inside lights go off as the last panel closes, and the fills never change. Diff frame 0 against the last frame.
- **The camera movement is unproven.**
  - Fix: glide at about 0.2 m/s with 3-5° of yaw drift. Keep wood grain, floor planks and the bed's drawer fronts in frame, and push S1 closer.
  - Run `hh_metrics.py` on a 720p preview of the first 7 s before the final render.

- **Also use**: `pipeline/figure.py` (`figure` with `seat`, `Figure.sit_pose`, `track` for the head turns, `animate` with `pop` and `visible`).
- **New pieces**:
  - **The folding wall**: `fold(u)` makes a zig-zag. Hinge k sits at `x0 + k·w·cos θ`, and odd hinges move `w·sin θ` out into the room. Panels rotate ±θ in turn, and the free end rides the top track. Then the stack moves 0.64 m north into the pocket. Play it backwards to close.
  - **Mechanisms**: two kidney shelves on a two-step path; the U-drawer (three boxes around a tube trap); the larder's five staggered inner drawers; the dishwasher door and racks; the hob lid and sink cover on runners.
  - **Fixtures**: the integrated fridge with its 80 mm filler and slatted grilles; the telescoping duct (`grow` on z); the storage bed with three drawers; the over-bed cupboard and book bay; the counter stool; the LED strips, hood light and bed wall light.
  - **Sound**: one new "boing" kind in music.py.
- **Effort**: medium-high. It is still all boxes, lathes and existing animation verbs, with no arm solve. One `project.py` can choose the 15 s or 46 s cut with an environment variable. If time is tight, drop the dishwasher and the over-bed cupboard beats first.
- **Hard to render, and how to avoid it**:
  - No running water.
  - No steam: show the duct instead.
  - No clear glass: frosted jars with coloured fill, solid fridge shelves, ceramic tumblers.
  - Simple lathe fruit and box packets. A laptop made of plain boxes, with no logo.
  - A mannequin figure with no face or hair and no walk: it pops in already seated.
  - Offset each panel by its thickness so the fold doesn't intersect.
- **Render and check**:
  - Probe each station with `ROOM_REST=1`, then probe the busy moments listed above. Render the 15 s cut first, and get it through `hh_metrics.py` before starting the long cut.
  - The README gives about 35 s per frame on 4 cores (720x1280). At that rate the 15 s cut (375 frames) takes about 3.6 h and the long cut (1,150 frames) about 11.2 h: about 15 h of CPU in all, before the upscale to 1080x1920. The camera never stops, so few frames can be copied.
