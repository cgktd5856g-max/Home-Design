# The Advent Stair: 8 m² kids' room (plan)

The coordinator switched the concept on 23 Nov (`production/sessions/advent_drawer_wall_update1.md`, the judged
winner C5). The folder and branch names are unchanged. This replaces the earlier drawer-wall plan; the toys, helpers,
music and snow carry over.

**Pitch.** An 8 m² kids' room builds itself around a loft bed whose staircase hides 24 small drawers. They ripple
open bottom to top, each on the next note of a rising scale and each presenting a tiny treasure. Then they snap shut
in a descending ripple. The child climbs to the last drawer for the star that tops the little tree in the loft, and
the fairy lights come on.

**Caption (post text, never on screen):** "8 m² kids' room. 6 stairs, 24 drawers: one for every day until
Christmas. What goes in the last one? Save it for next December." Debate question for the pinned comment: "Would
your kid wait 24 days or open them all on day one?" Post 27 Nov to 1 Dec (15 s cut).

## Rules this plan keeps
- No on-screen text. **No numbers or letters on the drawers.** The order comes from the ripple (bottom to top) and
  from the rising scale; the last drawer is the top riser.
- No characters, logos or IP. The treasures are generic: a wooden car, baubles, a duck, a candy cane, blocks, a bell,
  a rocket, a mitten, a teddy, gifts, a yo-yo, a mini tree, a sock, a drum, a sweet, a train, a wooden star, a top, a
  bunny, a gingerbread-style house. No snowmen or character figures.
- Original music only (sleigh bells and the scale are synthesized in code).
- White walls, a white stair with round oak knobs and oak treads, a light oak floor and loft. Festive colour stays
  tiny: one forest-green tree, a green throw and cushion, red only on small toys. The colour comes from the 24
  treasures, the books and the toy bins.
- Bright snowy daylight with a sunbeam. The warm fairy lights come on under daylight exposure, with no night switch.
- The stair is safe to argue for: fixed treads, with drawers only in the stepped side face and in the top risers.
- Festive props are separate pieces (tree, garland, baubles, star), so an evergreen version can be rendered fresh
  in January.

## Storage moments
1. **The 24-drawer cascade** (the hero): bottom to top, 0.12 s apart (2.8 s), each on the next note of a rising F
   major scale. All 24 are held open for about 1.5 s while the camera pushes along the stair, then they close in a
   descending ripple, 0.035 s apart.
2. **The tease**: at 1.0 s a single drawer drops its front with one high note and a glint, then shuts. It poses the
   open question the cascade answers.
3. **The top drawer** (the person moment): the child steps up, opens the top riser drawer and the star flies to the
   tree.
4. **Toy bins**: four fabric bins drop into the den cubby with toys peeking out.
5. **Desk drawer**: it slides out, pencils and paper drop in, and it shuts.
6. **Book wall**: books fill both shelves in a sweep of colour (red, mustard, white, green, sky, blue).
7. **Headboard cubby**: books and a torch land in it.
8. (long cut) **Under-landing wardrobe**: the door opens, small clothes fill the rail, two drawers fill, and it shuts.
9. (long cut) **Pull-out bottom step**.

## Long cut (about 44 s), built on the same `build()` with `ROOM_CUT=long`
0-6 s the opening, posts, deck, rail and bed; 6-13 s the stair blocks and the tease, then the den; 13-19 s the
under-landing wardrobe; 19-24 s the book wall and cubby; 24-32 s the cascade with the push-in (wow at about 60-70%);
32-39 s the child climbs, the top drawer and the star; 39 s lights on; 39-44 s the drifting final wide (2 s or more).
A new angle every 2-4 s and no end card.

## Sound
`rooms/advent_drawer_wall/music_advent.py`: 120 BPM in F major (pad, e-piano and bass from the shared synth), with
synthesized sleigh bells on 8ths and a glockenspiel figure in the intro and at lights-on. The cascade drawers play
an ascending F major scale (one note per drawer, per-event tick pitch) and the close ripple plays it back down.
`sfx_only` has the landing sounds and the scale but no music or bells.
