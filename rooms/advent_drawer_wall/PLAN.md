# Advent drawer wall: kids' room (plan)

Idea #2 from `research/trends_nov2026.md`: a kids' room that builds itself while snow falls outside, with a wall
of 24 oak drawers as the hero. The drawers open in a fast cascade and each one shows a small toy, ornament or
sweet. Post between 27 Nov and 3 Dec 2026; it can run all December.

**Evidence.** Kids' rooms run at 1.35x the account's normal views (report.md section 4). Christmas content starts
on 1 Nov, #cozychristmas is up 170% and advent calendars peak in early December (trends_nov2026.md). 24 reveals is
far more storage density than the hits' usual 6-12.

**Caption (post text, never on screen):** "24 drawers, one for every day until Christmas 🎄 What goes in drawer 24?"
plus "10 m² kids' room" for the size boost. Pin a comment asking for room requests with dimensions.

## Rules this plan keeps
- No on-screen text: **no numbers or letters on the drawers.** You tell the days apart by position (a 4 x 6 grid),
  by the rhythm of the cascade (a diagonal wave from bottom left to top right), and by what is inside (24
  different small items). The fronts are plain oak with a small white round knob.
- No characters, logos or recognisable IP. The toys are generic: a wooden car, a bauble, a wrapped sweet, a
  teddy, a star, a bell, a snowman, a candy cane, a gift box, a rocket, a block, a duck and so on.
- Original music made in code (sleigh-bell percussion, 120 BPM). No licensed Christmas songs.
- White shell, light oak, one accent: **forest green**, used on the window-seat cushion, the bed throw, a desk-chair
  pad, ribbons and the tree. The colour comes from what fills the storage (the drawer toys, the books, the plush
  toys in the bins). Red appears only as small toys and baubles, so saturation and warmth stay inside the hits' band.
- Clean and bright: a snowy daylight room. The finale goes to a blue-hour sky with warm lights inside, and the
  room stays well lit.

## Storage moments (numbered)
1. **Toy bins** roll out of the plinth under the drawer wall; plush toys and blocks drop in; the bins roll shut.
2. **Stair drawers** open in a cascade up the steps (slippers, books, a plush toy, crayons) and shut one by one.
3. **Desk drawer** slides out with pencils and a sketchbook, then closes.
4. **Window-seat drawers**: two big drawers open, fill with board games and a toy train, and close.
5. **Top flaps** over the drawer wall lift, wrapped gifts with green ribbons slide in, and they slam shut.
6. **The advent cascade** (the wow moment): all 24 drawers open in a diagonal wave in 1.2 s. Each toy hops up out of
   its drawer and settles. A hold while the camera drifts, then they close in the reverse wave.
7. **Drawer 24** (the stand-in person moment): a child stand-in pops in at the wall, reaches up, and one last drawer
   at the child's reach opens with a small green gift. It hops, and the child keeps the pose.
8. **The tree niche**: a slim tree slots into the niche with a thud and a star drops onto it.
9. (long cut) **Toy wardrobe**: the door pops open, small clothes slide in on the rail, and the door slams.
10. (long cut) **Book ledge**: face-out picture books land one by one.

## Long cut (about 45 s)
The same opening, then a new zone every 6-10 s and a new angle every 2-4 s:
0-4 s the hero build (as above); 4-10 s the loft and stair drawers (two angles); 10-16 s the desk under the loft
(drawer, shelf, books, lamp; two angles); 16-22 s the window seat with snow (two angles); 22-28 s the south toy
wardrobe and book ledge (two angles); 28-34 s the top flaps and gifts, then the tree; **30-33 s the advent cascade
(the wow, about 68% of the runtime)** and the child at drawer 24; 34-40 s a slow orbit across the open-and-closing
wall; 40-45 s blue hour, the lights cascade and a 2 s drifting wide that matches frame 1. No end card.
`ROOM_CUT=long` selects this timeline; `build()` is shared.

## Sound
An original cue in `rooms/advent_drawer_wall/music_advent.py`, built on `pipeline/music.py`:
- 120 BPM in F major (F, Dm, B-flat, C), with the pad, e-piano and bass from the shared synth.
- Sleigh bells on 8ths with accented off-beats (jingle clusters made of detuned high partials with a shake
  envelope).
- A glockenspiel figure in bars 1-2 and on the cascade.
- Every landing gets a sound: thuds for big pieces, ticks for small ones (pitches stepping up through the
  cascade), a swish for each opening, chimes for the lights.
- `sfx_only` has no music and no bells, so it can go under a trending sound on TikTok and Instagram.
