# What is working now: room-build trends, Aug-Nov 2026

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. This report adds to the Haven Haus study (`HANDOFF.md`, `report.md`). It does not
repeat that study's findings, except where a new fact confirms or changes one.

## How to read this

- **Labels**
  - **[fact]**: read from a source. The source and its date are given.
  - **[snapshot]**: the number comes from a search engine's copy of a page. These copies can lag the live page by
    weeks, and the copy date is usually unknown.
  - **[measured]**: worked out here. For example, a TikTok or Douyin video's upload date can be read from its ID:
    the ID shifted right by 32 bits gives Unix seconds. A Threads or Instagram post date can be read from its
    shortcode in the same way.
  - **[inference]**: my judgement.
- **Method**
  - About 75 web searches in English and Chinese.
  - Page fetches were blocked by this session's network settings. Homes & Gardens, Forbes, Behr and Young House
    Love all failed with a DNS error, so I stopped trying other sites. To read pages directly, those hosts have to
    be allowed in the session's network settings.
  - Every number below therefore comes from search-engine summaries and snapshots, not from pages read directly.
  - TikTok, Instagram, Douyin and Xiaohongshu pages need a login or refuse automated readers, so I did not open
    them.
- **Sample sizes** are given in each section. Most numbers here come from one source each. Read them as
  direction, not as precise values.

## Summary: the most useful findings

1. **The 3D layout genre is crowded and flat in 2026. The one topic that still breaks out is "two kids, one
   room".**
   - HomeCraft (@homecraft.designer, 1.1M followers, about 8M likes) is the biggest active 3D small-room account I
     found.
     - Its single-bedroom posts from Jan 2025 to May 2026 show 260-924 likes in snapshots.
     - Its 22 May 2026 post "Two kids, one room..." shows 22.5K likes.
     - Its best snapshot overall is from April 2024: 351.8K likes.
   - MK DESIGNS (@mkdesign.33, 217K followers) shows the same pattern. Its best post is "two beds in one small
     room" at 46.8K likes, while its other posts get 570-1,180 likes. [snapshot]
   - This matches the Haven Haus slump: in 2026, only kids' rooms and bedrooms with raised beds still win.
2. **The "empty white room gets filled" idea is a live trend right now, from AI.**
   - The ChatGPT prompt "fill this room with everything you know about me" spread in October 2026.
   - Threads posts about it are dated 11-13 Oct 2026 [measured]. There are TikTok discover pages in English and
     French, and a how-to guide dated November 2026 [snapshot].
   - Our frame 1 (an empty room) already matches this premise, and our version is buildable and original.
3. **Real tiny-home tours are where the big 2026 numbers are.**
   - A "smallest apartment in Paris" tour: nearly 7M views (17 May 2026).
   - A Seoul gosiwon tour: 1.92M views (Feb 2026).
   - A 7 m² Seoul room: 13M+ views. [fact, news coverage]
   - An architect fixing a viewer's tiny bedroom layout: 2.1M+ views in under two months. [fact]
   - Real-world, constraint-first premises ("9 m² attic, 134 stairs", "7 m² room") are the strongest premise
     family, and they back up the Haven Haus finding that titles naming a size do better.
4. **Christmas on social media now starts on 1 November, and "cosy" and "DIY" lead it.**
   - TikTok reports that US searches for "Christmas decorations" rose more than 40-fold from October to November
     2026.
   - Posts tagged #cozychristmas are up 170%+ and #diychristmas 80%+. #pinkchristmas is up 210%+, but it is off
     our colour band. [fact, TikTok newsroom]
   - Advent calendars peak in early December.
   - A 24-drawer advent wall is a storage reveal by its nature, and it needs no text.
5. **The 2027 colours of the year are mostly green, with some muted blue.**
   - Of the 7 paint-brand picks found, 4 are greens: Benjamin Moore Webster Green, Sherwin-Williams Celery, Behr
     Grounded and Dulux Soulful Sage. 2 are muted blues: Valspar Cottage Door and Glidden Artifact. [measured
     count; fact, brand releases via snapshots]
   - Pantone announces its 2027 colour in about 1-3 December, and 22 of 55 experts asked by Forbes predicted a
     green. Pantone's 2026 colour was its first white, Cloud Dancer.
   - Our band (white shell, light wood, one sage or muted-blue accent) is exactly on trend.
6. **Wood is overtaking white for cabinets, and whites are getting warmer.**
   - Houzz (via secondary sources): wood is the preferred cabinet finish for 29% of renovating homeowners, against
     28% for painted white.
   - This supports the earlier finding that our renders are too grey and cool. Use light oak fronts, not more white.
7. **AI fatigue is real, and the platforms are acting on it.**
   - Pinterest names home decor as one of the categories most prone to AI images, and lets users turn AI content
     down. TikTok has a similar control.
   - YouTube's July 2026 policy update reportedly singles out "generic or template-based" mass-produced videos
     (secondary sources, which conflict).
   - Unofficial Chinese articles report that Douyin is cutting reach for template-made, same-composition content.
   - Originals win on three points: real dimensions, physical believability, and a different layout each time.
     Never ship the same template twice.
8. **Sends, saves and rewatches have different drivers.**
   - Sends drive reach to people who don't follow you (Instagram's Adam Mosseri).
   - Content that inspires awe is shared more (Berger & Milkman, 2012).
   - Practical, dimensioned content gets saved: HomeCraft's 2026 captions say "📌 Save this".
   - YouTube counts every replay as a view, so a seamless loop pays off.
   - An older benchmark found that home Reels get more shares (489) than saves (317) on average.

- **One room, several uses.** Bed + office + cinema; a 3-in-1 wall.

- In these snapshots, a 2025-26 3D layout clip without a strong premise gets hundreds of likes. A clip with a

  data.
- The genre has not died. It has saturated, so the premise now matters more than the render.

### Signs of fatigue

[fact unless marked]

- **AI content**
  - Pinterest named home decor as a category "highly prone to AI modification or generation", added "AI modified"
    labels, and lets users turn GenAI content down by category, home décor included (TechCrunch, Tom's Guide,
    Quartz; 2025).
  - TikTok has an AI-generated-content control in Manage Topics and says it has labelled 1.3B+ clips as AI
    (Quartz and AP coverage; date unclear).
  - An Oct 2026 investigation found AI-generated TikTok rental videos that did not match the real listings (KSLA,
    27 Oct 2026).
  - EU AI transparency rules, including labels for AI-generated content, began rolling out in August 2026 (RTE and
    CP24, 20 Nov 2026).
  - China has required AI-content labels since 1 Sep 2025. Douyin adds a "suspected AI-generated, judge with care"
    label to unlabelled content it detects (Xinhua, IT之家).
- **Templates and repetition**
  - YouTube reportedly split its "inauthentic content" monetisation rule on 16 Jul 2026, and one of the new
    categories is "generic or template-based" videos. Secondary sources only, and one source describes the change
    differently.
  - Unofficial 2026 Chinese articles say Douyin treats "template repetition, same-composition reskins and highly
    similar topics" as low value, and cuts reach for accounts that repost or lightly re-edit.
  - Haven Haus's 2026 re-cuts flopped (earlier study).
- **Practicality critiques**
  - An architect called the viral TikTok tiny-home designs "impractical and unsafe" (Philstar, Jun 2024).
  - Haven Haus's top comments are practicality debates (earlier study).
- **Category-level engagement**: a search summary of 2026 benchmark pages says Rival IQ's 2025 report found Home
  Decor engagement on Instagram fell by nearly 30%. I did not see the original report, so treat this as low
  confidence.

### How originals can stand out

[inference, from the above]

1. **Look buildable.**
   - Real dimensions: put the size in the caption and a measured plan in the pinned comment.
   - Beds you can make, fridges with room to vent, doors that clear the furniture. This answers the commonest
     practicality attacks.
2. **Look physical, not AI.**
   - Pieces that land with weight and a sound, drawers on real runners, consistent scale.
   - Avoid what AI video gets wrong: melting objects, rooms that fill themselves, inconsistent geometry.
3. **Never the same template twice.** Change the room shell, the camera path, the music and the palette accent
   every video. Being original by construction is the moat; a repeated look throws it away.
4. **Make the premise human.** Who lives here (two kids, a couple, a nurse on nights, a cat), not just what is in
   the room.
5. **Say it is original in the comments, not on screen.** Pin "Modelled and rendered from code, no AI video, no
   design app."
   - YouTube says creators don't need to disclose clearly unrealistic or animated content (YouTube blog, 2024;
     Help Center).
   - Our renders are 3D animation, not AI-generated. Check the current Help Center wording if a render looks
     photo-real.

## 5. What gets these videos sent, saved and rewatched

### Facts

- **Instagram** (Adam Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025, restated in 2026 guides)
  - The three main ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach.
  - "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for
    unconnected content." Reels mostly reach non-followers, so sends matter most for growth.
  - Skip rate was added to Reels insights in August 2025. A share-rate metric was reportedly added in April 2026
    (one secondary source).
- **TikTok**: no official weights are published. Blogs agree on the direction: completion and rewatches first,
  then shares and saves above likes. The specific multipliers they quote ("shares 10x likes") have no source.
- **YouTube Shorts**: since 31 Mar 2025, every start or replay counts as a view. "Viewed vs swiped away" is the
  hook test shown in Studio. Third-party benchmarks conflict, and YouTube publishes no target.
- **Home content gets shared more than saved**: an older DashSocial benchmark (about two years old) put Home Reels
  at an average of 489 shares and 317 saves.
- **Awe drives sharing**: in a study of New York Times articles (Berger & Milkman, *Journal of Marketing Research*,
  2012), content evoking high-arousal positive emotions such as awe was shared more, even after controlling for
  practical usefulness.
- **Save prompts in the genre**: HomeCraft's 2026 captions end with "📌 Save this for your next bedroom reno".
- **Chinese platforms**: marketing sources say "strong solution" content (a clear, usable plan you come back to)

### Inferences: design for each action

| Action | What triggers it | How to build it in, with no on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| **Send** (to a person) | "This is us" (siblings, a couple, a roommate), awe at density, an argument starter | Premises that name a household: two kids, a couple with a baby, a nurse on nights. A caption that invites a send: "Send this to whoever gets the top bunk." A contestable feature: a bed over the desk, a bath under the floor |
| **Save** | Practical, measurable, copyable | The size in the caption ("2.7 × 3.3 m, 2.6 m ceiling"); a plan with dimensions in the pinned comment; one hero trick viewers can copy (toe-kick step drawers, a 30 cm pull-out pantry, an over-door rack); the caption ends "Save for your reno" |
| **Rewatch** | Too much to catch in one view; a seamless loop | 6-12 reveals, some happening at the same time (both kids' sides build at once). | **Comment** | Practicality debates, requests | Answer the practicality debate in the design (a vent gap behind the fridge, a ladder angle), then ask a question in the caption: "Would you sleep up there?" Pin requests and answer them as a series |

### Things to avoid

1. **Saturated festive palettes**: pink Christmas, tartan, heavy burgundy or chocolate walls.
   - Use one festive accent at most.
   - In the earlier study, off-band colour marked 13 of 25 flops but only 2 of 25 hits.
2. **A repeated template.** No two videos with the same shell, camera path, track or accent.
   - Platform rules penalise template-made, repeated content: YouTube July 2026 (secondary sources), Douyin
     (unofficial articles).
   - Haven Haus's 2026 re-cuts flopped.
3. **Anything that looks AI-made**: self-filling rooms without landings, warping objects, AI-generated people.
   - Pinterest and TikTok now let users filter AI content.
   - Stay clearly 3D-animated and physical.
4. **Single-piece videos**: a gift-wrap cabinet alone, a cat cabinet alone, an advent box alone. Always build the
   whole room around the star feature.
5. **Claims that don't hold up**: calling it an "apartment" with no kitchen or bathroom, or a "9 m²" room that is
   visibly bigger. A pile-on brings reach, but it costs trust.
6. **Licensed Christmas songs.** Trending tracks like "Last Christmas" may not be available on business accounts
   (NewEngen), and the owner's rule is original music.
7. **On-screen text, including "before/after" labels.** Show the problem through motion and sound instead.
